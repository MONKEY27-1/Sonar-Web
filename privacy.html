Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026.

Sonar (the desktop app) works fully offline without an account. This page covers what's collected only if you choose to create a Sonar account, either in the app or on this website.

What we collect

  • Account info: the username, email address, and password you provide when registering. Passwords are handled entirely by Supabase Auth (our authentication provider) — we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • License/tier status: whether your account is Free, Beta Tester, or Pro, so the app and site can unlock the right features.
  • Support messages: anything you send us through the in-app or website support chat.
  • Payment (Pro upgrade only): handled entirely by Stripe's own hosted checkout. We never receive or store your card number — Stripe sends us only a confirmation that a payment succeeded.

What we don't collect

Sonar doesn't track your sound library contents, hotkeys, or usage analytics. The desktop app's only outbound network calls are checking GitHub Releases for updates and, if you're signed in, syncing your account/license status.

How it's stored

Account data lives in our Supabase project (hosted on Supabase's infrastructure). Your desktop app session is kept in your browser's local storage on this site, and in a local settings file on your PC for the app itself.

Your data, your call

You can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings → Account in the app, or by contacting Support.

Questions

Reach us through Support or open an issue on GitHub.
