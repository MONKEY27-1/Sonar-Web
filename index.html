Everything a soundboard should do

Built with .NET 8 and NAudio for a lightweight, low-latency desktop app — not a browser wrapper.

🎵

Unlimited imports

Drag and drop MP3, WAV, OGG, or FLAC files. Everything's copied into your own local library — nothing streamed, nothing rented.

⚡

Low-latency playback

WASAPI-based audio engine plays multiple sounds simultaneously with minimal delay, tuned for real-time use in games.

🎤

Multi-device audio routing

Send sounds to several headphone outputs and capture from several microphones at once, with automatic mic passthrough and real loudness normalization — not just a single virtual cable.

⌃

Global hotkeys

Low-level keyboard/mouse hooks trigger sounds even while a game has focus — including a dedicated Stop All hotkey.

🎭

Voice changer

Live pitch, formant, and true tempo shifting (duration stays independent of pitch), plus robot, echo, distortion, overdrive, delay, reverb, and proximity effects — with saved voice presets.

📦

Plugin marketplace

Browse community plugins and packs — hotkey sets, voice presets, and themes — shared by other Sonar users.

⭐

Organize your board

Favorites, folders, tags, and instant search keep a big library fast to navigate mid-match.

🎨

Dark, Light & AMOLED

Built-in themes plus full custom theming, so Sonar matches the rest of your setup.

↻

Auto-updates

Sonar checks GitHub Releases on launch and offers a one-click install for new versions — no manual re-downloads.

🚀

Performance Mode

Reduces visual effects, virtualizes the sound library, and lowers background polling — keeps large libraries fast on lower-end hardware.

🔧

Advanced Settings

Real RMS-based loudness normalization across your whole library, plus deeper diagnostics — no more guessing why one sound plays louder than the rest.
